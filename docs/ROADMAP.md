# Product roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Branding, responsive homepage, catalogue search and product detail.
- FastAPI health, search, product, comparison and calculator endpoints.
- PostgreSQL core schema and seed records.

## Phase 2 — Trusted comparison
- Authorised store adapters and scheduled feed ingestion.
- Canonical product + variant matching and manual review queue.
- Price freshness, historical records and graph.

## Phase 3 — Savings tools
- Coupon verification and bank-offer eligibility rules.
- Final payable price calculator.
- Authenticated wishlist and price alerts with email/push delivery.

## Phase 4 — Chrome extension
- Store-specific detection adapters and robust manual fallback.
- Popup and side panel backed by the same API.
- Privacy review, permission minimisation and Chrome Web Store packaging.

## Phase 5 — Marketplace
- Transferability checks, seller verification, moderation, payment provider integration.
- Buyer protection, order states, disputes and audit trail.
- Legal and issuer-term review before enabling coupon resale.

## Phase 6 — Production operations
- Authentication, RBAC, rate limits, observability, backups and incident response.
- Automated tests, CI/CD, migrations, security review and accessibility.
- Production deployment, privacy policy, terms and support process.
